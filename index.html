Talk time SLA

Pace (days worked) is talk time to date divided by the required daily rate times that CSM's own active days, so 100% means on target for the days they worked. Pace (calendar) divides by every working day elapsed in the quarter for that CSM's team, so it also reflects time away. A large gap between the two points to attendance rather than productivity. Projected quarter assumes the CSM keeps their daily rate across every working day in the quarter; it is an estimate, not a forecast of attendance.
